#+TITLE: I Almost Bought a Pipe Today
#+AUTHOR: Paco Pascal

I almost bought a pipe online today. But then my inner voice yelled
back, "No dammit!"

That little man between my ears was right. I can't buy a tobacco pipe
online. I need to go into a tobacco shop where there awaits a dirty
old man. He waits to show me his domain, expressing the aesthetics of
each pipe. The flavor of each tobacco. We'll climb around his tiny
shop. And as we walk down each aisle, little bits of dried grease will
be pulled by the wind from his hair. He'll tell a rude joke. And I'll
point to the pipe and say, "That's the one."

That's how. That's how I'll buy a pipe.
